Search for Unclaimed Property

The Department of the Treasury holds millions of dollars in unclaimed funds from forgotten bank accounts, uncashed checks, insurance policies, and safe deposit boxes. Search our official database below to verify if funds are owed to you.

Secure Verification Notice

Our division ensures all claims undergo rigorous authentication to prevent identity theft and fraud. There is never a fee to search for or claim your properties through this official Treasury branch.
